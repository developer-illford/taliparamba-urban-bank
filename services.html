Safe Deposit Locker

A secure lockable storage space provided by banks to their customers for keeping valuable items and important documents safe. These lockers are designed to protect assets such as jewelry, property deeds, wills, certificates, rare collectibles, and other personal valuables from theft, fire, or natural disasters.

Key Features:
  • High Security: Bank lockers are housed within a strong, guarded vault with round-the-clock surveillance and restricted access.
  • Confidentiality: Only the account holder (or authorized nominees) can access the locker, ensuring complete privacy.
  • Variety of Sizes: Lockers are available in different sizes to accommodate items of varying dimensions.
  • Easy Access: Customers can access their lockers during the bank’s working hours, following proper identification procedures.
  • Peace of Mind: Storing valuables in a bank locker reduces the risk associated with keeping them at home.

A Safe Deposit Locker is an ideal solution for anyone looking to safeguard their valuable possessions securely and conveniently.

RTGS / NEFT

RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement) and NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer) are electronic fund transfer systems that allow customers to transfer money securely from one bank account to another across India. These services offer a fast, reliable, and convenient way to send and receive funds.

Key Features:
  • RTGS: Transfers are processed in real-time for high-value transactions, ensuring immediate settlement.
  • NEFT: Transfers are processed in hourly batches, suitable for both small and large amounts.
  • Secure Transactions: Funds are transferred safely through the bank’s electronic network.
  • Convenient: Initiate transfers online, via mobile banking, or at branch counters.
  • 24/7 Access: NEFT is available round the clock, including weekends and holidays (for online transfers), while RTGS is available during bank working hours.

CTS Clearing

CTS (Cheque Truncation System) Clearing is an electronic process for clearing cheques between banks. Instead of physically moving cheques, CTS allows banks to transfer cheque images and data electronically, ensuring faster and more secure settlement.

Key Features:
  • Faster Clearing: Cheque settlement is much quicker as physical movement of cheques is eliminated.
  • Secure Process: Electronic images of cheques reduce the risk of loss, fraud, or damage during transit.
  • Accurate Settlement: Automated processing minimizes errors in cheque clearing.
  • Convenient for Customers: Cheques are credited to the beneficiary account faster, improving fund availability.
  • Standardized System: CTS follows RBI guidelines, ensuring uniformity across all banks in India.

CTS Clearing is an efficient and secure method for processing cheques, offering speed and reliability to both banks and customers.
